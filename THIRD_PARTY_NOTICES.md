# Component licensing and attribution

This consolidated repository does not grant a blanket license over all components.

- PrOntoQA original experiment code retains its [MIT license](experiments/prontoqa/LICENSE). Its generator retains [Apache-2.0](experiments/prontoqa/vendor/prontoqa/LICENSE); see [NOTICE](experiments/prontoqa/NOTICE.md).
- Behavioral evaluations retain their [component-specific notices](experiments/behavior/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), dataset cards and upstream licenses.
- SSP retains its upstream references and existing licensing status; consolidation does not add a license to formerly unlicensed source.
- Model access, dataset redistribution and paper figures remain governed by their respective publishers.

Retained third-party sources are pinned in the migration inventory or component provenance. Historical source hash records remain evidence of their original snapshots; current publication hashes are recorded separately.
