- **SSP:** upstream references and the source's existing licensing status are retained. This study does not add a license to formerly unlicensed source.

Retained experimental inputs and results cite the upstream datasets; paper figures retain CC BY 4.0 attribution. See [experiment notices](experiments/truth-geometry/NOTICE.md). No new license is granted to upstream source.
- **Models, datasets and paper figures:** access, use and redistribution remain governed by their respective publishers.

Scientific source attribution is retained in each experiment. Provenance is not a replacement for a license grant.
